About

I'm Ting, an MSc graduate in Communication Science (Cum Laude) from the University of Amsterdam, where I studied at the Amsterdam School of Communication Research (ASCoR). My work sits at the intersection of health communication and human–AI interaction: I'm interested in when people trust what an AI tells them about their health, and why an agent that holds its ground under pushback is trusted more than one that simply agrees.

My MSc thesis, Medicine or Mirror? How LLM Sycophancy Shapes Epistemic Trust in Health Advice, tested this directly with a 2×2 experiment on an LLM health consultant I built from scratch. Before that, I completed a BSc in Communication Science, also at the University of Amsterdam, and worked as a research assistant on eye-tracking, nudge-design, and computational content analysis projects.

I'm currently applying for PhD positions in computer science and communication science, and collaborating with Professor Mengxue Ou at Peking University on a person-centered study of how people's normative perceptions of generative AI in health contexts cluster into distinct profiles.
